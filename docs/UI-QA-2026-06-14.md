situation. Illustrations are real Gemini-generated assets (all 33 referenced

The defects below are mostly **P2 polish**; a couple of **P1** items are worth
fixing. I fixed the safe P1s I was confident about and left the rest as a
checklist (UI runtime verification was blocked — see "Verification" at the end).

## What I checked (and found clean)

- **RTL correctness**: `dir="rtl"` on inputs, logical `start-*`/`text-end`,
  conditional arrow flipping in blog/contact/terms/privacy, accent bars placed
  at the RTL start edge (`right-0`). No bidi/overflow bugs spotted in code.
- **Empty/loading/error states**: skeletons (`TaskListSkeleton`), empty-state
  illustrations (`empty-tasks`, `empty-shopping`, `empty-stats`, `empty-weekly`,
  `tasks-done`), `(app)/error.tsx` + `(app)/loading.tsx` exist.
- **Animations**: transform/opacity only; **no** width/height/top/left/margin
  transitions in `globals.css`. Framer `layoutId` for nav, spring transitions.
- **Accessibility**: `aria-label`/`aria-current`/`aria-pressed`,
  `focus-visible:ring`, meaningful `alt` on every content image, `aria-hidden`
  on decorative ones.
- **Art**: all 33 illustration references resolve to real files; consistent
  warm flat style; no placeholders/broken images.
